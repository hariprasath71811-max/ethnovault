Our promise

Knowledge belongs
to its keepers.

EthnoVault is not a museum. It is a set of agreements — with elders, linguists and families — about what may be recorded, who may listen, and how a story should travel.

  • Consent before collection

    Free, prior and informed consent is documented for every single record — and can be withdrawn.

  • Community owns the record

    Attribution and licensing stay with the originating community. We are guests, not owners.

  • Living, never frozen

    Records are updated as language and practice evolve. A living archive listens back.

Community verification

3-elder quorum

The council keeps it honest.

New records need three endorsements from elders or culture-bearers of the originating community. Endorsing is a signal of trust, not a public vote.

Demonstration queue. Endorsements here simulate council review. In production, verification happens offline in community, with elders holding the final say.
